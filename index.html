How it works

No slides required, no formal discussant, no judgement. Just a good meal and good questions.

  1. Someone brings an idea

    A paper draft, a puzzling result, a research question you are not sure about yet, or a revise-and-resubmit you need to think through.

  2. We eat and talk

    A short presentation over food, then an open conversation. Junior and senior members around the same table.

  3. You leave with something

    New angles on theory, data, methods or framing, and people to follow up with afterwards.
